## 新增功能详解

### 1. 北向资金 (North Flow) ⭐

```python
# 北向资金总体
akshare.stock_hsgt_em()

# 沪股通
akshare.stock_em_hsgt_north_net_flow_in(indicator="沪股通")

# 深股通
akshare.stock_em_hsgt_north_net_flow_in(indicator="深股通")

# 详细数据
akshare.stock_em_hsgt_north_history(indicator="北向资金")
```

**触发词**：
- "北向资金"
- "外资流入"
- "沪股通"
- "深股通"
